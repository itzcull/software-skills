# Shape Up

## Sources and scope

- [Basecamp: Map the Scopes](https://basecamp.com/shapeup/3.3-chapter-12).
- [Basecamp: Show Progress](https://basecamp.com/shapeup/3.4-chapter-13).

These chapters describe the building portion of Shape Up, not its entire shaping, betting, or closure process. Teams discover scopes through work, prefer integrated pieces that can be finished independently, and distinguish resolving unknowns from executing a known solution. Scope boundaries can change when they obscure progress.

## Mapping to the abstract procedure

This is our interpretation using the [contract and evidence labels](../SKILL.md#researched-examples).

| Responsibility | Mapping and limits |
| --- | --- |
| [1. Intent and boundaries](../SKILL.md#1-establish-intent-and-boundaries) | **Partial:** the chapters assume an outline inherited from shaping. They do not establish how goals, boundaries, and authority were agreed. |
| [2. Structure the work](../SKILL.md#2-structure-the-work) | **Supported:** discover tasks, group them into meaningful scopes, and redraw boundaries as relationships become clearer. |
| [3. Prepare the next increment](../SKILL.md#3-select-and-prepare-the-next-increment) | **Partial:** sequence important, uncertain work early. Explicit evaluation criteria for a selected increment need a concrete provision. |
| [4. Execute](../SKILL.md#4-execute-the-increment) | **Supported:** build integrated pieces and validate approaches through hands-on work rather than relying only on a theory of the solution. |
| [5. Evaluate](../SKILL.md#5-evaluate-the-result) | **Partial:** working slices and validated approaches provide evidence of progress. Hill positions are status judgments, not substitutes for correctness or acceptance checks. |
| [6. Reconcile and adapt](../SKILL.md#6-reconcile-and-adapt) | **Supported:** inspect stalled scopes, resolve unknowns, split mixed scopes, and distinguish essential work from nice-to-haves. |
| [7. Close the scope](../SKILL.md#7-close-the-agreed-scope) | **Not established:** these chapters do not establish the overall acceptance and handoff procedure. Do not infer it from scopes being marked complete. |

## What remains implementation-specific

Scope maps and hill charts are particular mechanisms for organising work and communicating uncertainty. These chapters allow progress on several scopes and recommend resolving critical unknowns before finishing routine details. They do not imply strict completion of one atomic behaviour before any other work begins.

An adaptation needs explicit verification, authority, and closure provisions; consult the relevant remaining Shape Up chapters before describing its full delivery process.

**Lesson for the abstraction:** allow emergent decomposition and risk-reducing work; distinguish understanding a solution from finishing and verifying it.
